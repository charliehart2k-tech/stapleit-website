What threats face UK businesses and charities today?
Cyber threats are evolving faster than ever. Here's what your business is up against — and why the right protection matters.
Phishing & email fraud
Fake emails designed to trick your staff into handing over passwords, clicking dangerous links or transferring money. The most common attack method targeting UK businesses — and getting more convincing with AI.
Ransomware
Malicious software that locks your files and demands payment to restore access. Ransomware attacks on UK businesses doubled in 2025 and can bring your entire operation to a standstill within minutes.
Business email compromise
Attackers impersonate your CEO, a supplier or a trusted contact to trick staff into making fraudulent payments or sharing sensitive information. One of the most financially damaging threats facing small businesses.
Malware & viruses
Malicious software that infects your devices, steals data, monitors activity or disrupts your systems. Often installed without anyone realising — through a dodgy download, a USB drive or a compromised website.
Data breaches
Unauthorised access to your customer records, financial data or confidential files. A data breach can result in significant fines under GDPR, lasting reputational damage and loss of client trust.
Weak passwords & credential theft
Stolen or guessed passwords are one of the easiest ways into a business. Without multi-factor authentication, a single compromised password can give attackers access to your entire environment.
Unpatched devices & software
Outdated operating systems and applications are full of known vulnerabilities that attackers actively exploit. Regular patching is one of the most effective ways to keep your business protected.
Social engineering
Manipulating people rather than systems — phone calls, fake IT support, impersonation and psychological pressure to get staff to hand over access or information. No technical knowledge required from the attacker.
