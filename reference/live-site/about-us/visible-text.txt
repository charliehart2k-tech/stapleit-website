About Staple IT
Our qualified team has spent years working for large IT managed service providers supporting businesses across the UK.
The common issue we saw with these providers was that value, quality of service, and personal attention were slowly disappearing in favour of big contracts, lengthy terms, and unnecessary services.
Staple was created to bring this personal service back into IT at rates that business owners can afford.
We are a small team (just three of us!), just like many of our clients, but we share years of professional experience and are all fully qualified and insured.
We believe IT is a staple of your business and it should not be complicated or expensive – hence our name.
