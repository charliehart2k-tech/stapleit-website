Who do we support?
We help a wide variety of different organisations, providing them with great IT support for the people that make Surrey tick.
Small & Medium Sized Businesses
Staple provides proactive remote IT support for businesses that need help without the fuss. We fix the majority of issues remotely without any need for an on-site visit, and we proactively keep your devices secure and monitored so you're always protected. From setting up a new internet line to Microsoft 365 administration, we've got you covered.
Charities & non-profit Organisations
We know money can be a blocker, and you want to help as many people as possible without being left behind. We partner with Microsoft to help eligible organisations access charity pricing and reduce their software costs. From day-to-day IT support to Microsoft 365 setup, we'll make sure your technology works as hard as you do for your cause.
Sole traders & Freelancers
For freelancers who need to work from anywhere, we provide secure remote solutions to boost your productivity and keep you flexible and efficient wherever you go. With cloud systems tailored to support your work, we help you stay focused on your trade — not your technology. From email and file storage to remote access, we've got everything covered.
