Welcome to Staple IT

Are we online?

Surrey's trusted IT support provider for businesses, charities and individuals.

Who do we support?

We help a wide variety of different organisations, providing them with great IT support for the people that make Surrey tick.

Small & Medium Sized Businesses

Staple provides proactive remote IT support for businesses that need help without the fuss. We fix the majority of issues remotely without any need for an on-site visit, and we proactively keep your devices secure and monitored so you're always protected. From setting up a new internet line to Microsoft 365 administration, we've got you covered.

Charities & non-profit Organisations

We know money can be a blocker, and you want to help as many people as possible without being left behind. We partner with Microsoft to help eligible organisations access charity pricing and reduce their software costs. From day-to-day IT support to Microsoft 365 setup, we'll make sure your technology works as hard as you do for your cause.

Sole traders & Freelancers

For freelancers who need to work from anywhere, we provide secure remote solutions to boost your productivity and keep you flexible and efficient wherever you go. With cloud systems tailored to support your work, we help you stay focused on your trade — not your technology. From email and file storage to remote access, we've got everything covered.

Are we right for you?

What we can do for you.

From day-to-day IT support to cloud solutions and Microsoft 365, we provide everything your business needs to keep running — all under one roof, with no hidden costs and no unnecessary extras.

IT Support

View Support Services

We provide unlimited remote IT support backed by proactive monitoring, real-time alerts and automated patching across your entire device estate.

Covering OS updates and third-party applications on Windows, Mac and server environments. We remotely manage and maintain your devices, fixing issues quickly and keeping threats at bay before they become problems. 

From a single laptop to a full office setup, you get a dedicated support partner who knows your business and is always on hand when you need them.

IT Solutions

View Solutions Services

From initial deployment and design through to ongoing administration, we manage your entire cloud and technology environment so it works the way your business needs it to.

 Whether it's device management, identity, compliance, virtual desktops or cloud storage, we make sure your setup is secure, organised and built around you.

Our IT solutions are based around your business and your needs — we will never suggest something that doesn't fit or isn't needed.

 Everything we recommend has a purpose and adds value.

IT Consultancy

View Consultancy Services

From IT planning and roadmaps to AI integrations, Cyber Essentials, compliance and Microsoft 365 strategy, we provide honest, straightforward advice tailored to your business.

 Whether you're looking to modernise your setup, meet compliance requirements or simply figure out what you actually need, we'll take the time to understand your business and point you in the right direction. 

No buzz words, no unnecessary recommendations — just clear, practical guidance from people who know what they're talking about.

Cyber Security

View Cyber Security Services

From tenant security reviews and device hardening to Cyber Essentials, EDR, ITDR, MDR and antivirus, we provide layered protection that keeps your business safe from the threats that matter. 

We assess your entire environment — including physical security — and identify the gaps before someone else does.

Keeping your business secure doesn't have to be complicated, and we'll make sure you have the right protection in place without paying for things you don't need.

Why Choose & Trust Us.

We set up Staple because we saw too many businesses and charities paying too much for IT support that didn't deliver. Straightforward, affordable and personal — that's how we do things differently.

Qualified & experienced

Our engineers are fully qualified, experienced and insured, with years spent working for large IT providers supporting businesses across the UK. You get that same level of professional expertise behind your IT — without the big MSP price tag.

 Personal service

You'll always deal with someone who knows your business and your setup. No call centres in far away lands, no long ticket queues and no being passed around — just a real person who picks up the phone, answers emails and gets things done.

No lengthy contracts

We start on simple monthly agreements with no long-term lock-in and no lengthy commitments. We earn your business every single month, which means we're always motivated to deliver the best possible service.

Local to Epsom, Surrey

We're based in Epsom and focused on supporting the businesses and communities around us. When you need us on-site, we're never far away — and we're invested in the local area just like you are.

Some of our partners

We work with industry-leading technology partners to make sure you always get the best tools, the best protection and the best service behind your IT. 

As a Microsoft partner we bring enterprise-level cloud and productivity solutions to businesses of every size.
