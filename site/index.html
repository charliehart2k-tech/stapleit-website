Who do we support?

We help a wide variety of organisations with straightforward IT support for the people and businesses that make Surrey tick.

Charities & non-profit Organisations

Technology should help your cause, not drain the budget. We help eligible organisations access charity pricing and provide dependable day-to-day support, Microsoft 365 and cloud services.

Small & Medium Sized Businesses

Proactive remote IT support without the fuss. We resolve most issues remotely while keeping your devices secure, monitored and up to date — from Microsoft 365 administration to connectivity.

Sole traders & Freelancers

Secure, flexible IT for people who need to work from anywhere. From email and cloud storage to remote access, we keep technology out of the way so you can focus on your work.

Why trust Staple IT?

Qualified & experienced

Our engineers are fully qualified, experienced and insured, with years spent working for large IT providers supporting businesses across the UK. You get that same level of professional expertise behind your IT — without the big MSP price tag.

Personal service

You'll always deal with someone who knows your business and your setup. No call centres in far away lands, no long ticket queues and no being passed around — just a real person who picks up the phone, answers emails and gets things done.
