What we can do for you.

What we can do for you.

Technology that fits the way you work.

01

Microsoft 365

From tenant setup and licence management to Intune, Entra ID, security policies and compliance — we administer and optimise your entire Microsoft 365 environment so it's secure, organised and working the way your business needs it to.

02

Google Workspace

Whether you're migrating from another platform or need ongoing administration, we manage your entire Google Workspace environment — users, groups, security settings and everything in between — keeping your team connected and productive.

03

Cloud storage

Secure, accessible and properly organised cloud storage for your business — whether that's OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive or another platform. We set it up, migrate your existing data and make sure the right people have the right access.

04

Hosted & virtual desktops

Work from anywhere with Windows 365 Cloud PCs or Azure Virtual Desktop — fully managed, secure and ready to use on any device. We handle the deployment, configuration and ongoing management so your team can work flexibly without compromising on performance or security.

05

Managed software solutions

Running business-critical software like Sage 50, IRIS or Brightpay? We make sure these applications are properly hosted, maintained and supported — so your finance, payroll and accounting tools are always available and running without disruption.

06

Mobile device management

Keep every phone, tablet and laptop in your business secure and compliant using Intune. We enrol, configure and manage your devices remotely — enforcing security policies, ensuring compliance and giving you the ability to remotely wipe a lost or stolen device instantly.

07

Apple Business

We manage and secure your Apple devices using Apple Business Manager — enrolling iPhones, iPads and Macs into your environment, managing Apple IDs and making sure every device is locked down, compliant and ready to use straight out of the box.

08

SharePoint & collaboration

We design, build and manage SharePoint environments that actually work for your business — from intranet pages and document libraries to team sites and automated workflows. A properly built SharePoint saves time, reduces email and keeps your team on the same page.

09

Cloud migrations

Moving to the cloud for the first time or switching platforms? We plan and manage your migration from start to finish — minimising downtime, protecting your data and making sure everything lands in the right place without disruption to your business.

10

Licence reviews & cost optimisation

Are you paying for licences your team aren't using? We audit your entire Microsoft and cloud licence estate, identify waste and make sure you're only paying for what you actually need — often saving businesses a significant amount every month.
