Our approach

Practical AI

What could AI do for your business?

Since ChatGPT launched in 2022, AI models have been performing complex and detailed tasks in a matter of seconds — and they're only getting more capable. AI can now assist with daily tasks such as drafting emails, producing comprehensive documents, summarising important meetings and processing unstructured text into valuable business assets like detailed spreadsheets and charts.

However, all of these capabilities require careful consideration — you need to be confident that the data you share with any AI tool is data you are comfortable sharing.

Our three step process

Our three step process

01

Discover.

We sit down with you and explore your data, your workflows and your goals. We take the time to understand what you have and what you could pair with AI — so every recommendation starts from a place of genuine understanding.

02

Secure.

Before any AI tool touches your data, we make sure it's properly protected. We work with you to secure your data and ensure nothing ends up in an AI processing tool where it shouldn't be — getting this right is the foundation that makes everything else work safely.

03

Integrate.

Once we know what you need and your data is secure, we integrate the right AI tools into your business. The goal is to make the experience seamless — so it feels less like new technology and more like it was always part of the furniture.

A sensible first question

Is AI right for my business?

We get it — AI right now is a bit of a buzzword. You don't want to miss out, but you certainly don't want something you might not even need.

As part of our three-step process we will perform a complete assessment to understand whether AI is going to genuinely benefit your business or whether it's worth parking for now.

We will never recommend something we don't feel is needed, and certainly not something that won't add value or will cause you unnecessary headaches. Get in touch with our team today to discuss AI in depth for your business.
