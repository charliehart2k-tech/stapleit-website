What you're up against

What threats face UK businesses and charities today?

Cyber threats are evolving faster than ever. Here's what your business is up against — and why the right protection matters.

Phishing & email fraud

Fake emails designed to trick your staff into handing over passwords, clicking dangerous links or transferring money. The most common attack method targeting UK businesses — and getting more convincing with AI.

Ransomware

Malicious software that locks your files and demands payment to restore access. Ransomware attacks on UK businesses doubled in 2025 and can bring your entire operation to a standstill within minutes.

Business email compromise

Attackers impersonate your CEO, a supplier or a trusted contact to trick staff into making fraudulent payments or sharing sensitive information. One of the most financially damaging threats facing small businesses.

Malware & viruses

Malicious software that infects your devices, steals data, monitors activity or disrupts your systems. Often installed without anyone realising — through a dodgy download, a USB drive or a compromised website.

Data breaches

Unauthorised access to your customer records, financial data or confidential files. A data breach can result in significant fines under GDPR, lasting reputational damage and loss of client trust.

Weak passwords & credential theft

Stolen or guessed passwords are one of the easiest ways into a business. Without multi-factor authentication, a single compromised password can give attackers access to your entire environment.

Unpatched devices & software

Outdated operating systems and applications are full of known vulnerabilities that attackers actively exploit. Regular patching is one of the most effective ways to keep your business protected.

Social engineering

Manipulating people rather than systems — phone calls, fake IT support, impersonation and psychological pressure to get staff to hand over access or information. No technical knowledge required from the attacker.

How we protect you

How we protect you and what's important.

From endpoint protection and email security to Cyber Essentials certification and ongoing monitoring. We put the right layers of protection in place so you can focus on running your business.

Endpoint detection & response (EDR)

Industry-leading EDR deployed and managed across all your devices — detecting, containing and responding to threats in real time before they cause damage to your business.

Email security & anti-phishing

Advanced email filtering that stops malicious messages before they reach your inbox — blocking phishing attempts, malware, impersonation attacks and spam at the source.

Multi-factor authentication

An essential extra layer of protection ensuring a stolen password alone is never enough to access your systems, email or cloud environment.

Device hardening & configuration

We lock down your devices to industry best practice — disabling unnecessary features, enforcing security policies and making sure every endpoint is configured to reduce your attack surface.

Managed detection & response (MDR)

Around-the-clock monitoring of your environment — our tools watch for suspicious activity, unusual behaviour and active threats so we can respond fast before anything escalates.

Cyber Essentials certification

We guide you through the entire Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certification process — helping you meet government-backed security standards and demonstrate your commitment to protecting your business.

Identity & access management

We make sure the right people have access to the right systems — and nothing more. Using Entra ID and modern identity tools, we protect your business from identity-based attacks and insider threats.

Security awareness & guidance

Your people are your first line of defence. We provide clear, practical security guidance to help your team spot threats, stay vigilant and know exactly what to do if something doesn't look right.
