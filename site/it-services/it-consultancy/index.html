Consultancy areas

Advice grounded in what your business actually needs.

01

Microsoft

As a Microsoft partner we provide consultancy to help you get the most from Microsoft 365, Azure and related services. We offer clear, practical advice on setup, security, licensing and optimisation — ensuring your Microsoft environment is secure, efficient and built for your business.

02

Cyber security

We provide applied cyber security consultancy to help protect your business from modern threats. We can support you through Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certification, advise on security hygiene and identify what may be missing from your current setup.

03

Business continuity

Your business data is the beating heart of your organisation. We provide clear, transparent guidance on backup, disaster recovery and continuity planning — helping you reduce risk and making sure your business can keep running no matter what happens.

04

IT planning & strategy

We deliver IT strategy and planning consultancy to make sure your technology supports your business goals. From structured roadmaps to informed decision making, we help you plan for growth, reduce risk and avoid unnecessary costs.

05

Data migration

Moving your data from one platform to another doesn't have to be disruptive. We plan and execute migrations carefully to minimise downtime, ensure data integrity and get your team up and running on the new system as quickly as possible.

06

IT infrastructure audit

We provide infrastructure and IT audit services to give you a clear view of your technology environment. We review your systems, networks and security — identifying inefficiencies and delivering practical recommendations to optimise performance and reliability.

07

AI integration

Not sure where AI fits into your business? We help you identify where tools like Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise and Claude can add genuine value — cutting through the hype to find practical, secure solutions that make a real difference to how your team works.

08

Cost reviews

Are you getting value from your current technology spend? We review your entire IT estate — licences, subscriptions, hardware and services — identifying waste and recommending changes that could save your business a significant amount every month, without compromising on what you actually need.

Why Staple?

Why choose Staple for consultancy?

At Staple we believe that every business owner and key stakeholder deserves to understand the technology that runs their organisation — without needing a computer science degree to follow along.

We explain complex concepts in plain English, lay out your options clearly and never recommend something you don't genuinely need.

We won't be explaining the workings of a Turbo Encabulator any time soon — just honest, straightforward advice from people who know what they're talking about.
