Automated OS patching

Fixed monthly pricing

Getting started is simple

From your first conversation to fully managed IT support, we make the whole process straightforward — no technical knowledge required.

01

Get in touch

Give us a call, send us an email or fill in our contact form and we'll get back to you.

We'll have a friendly, no-obligation conversation about your business, your current IT setup and what you actually need.

02

We audit your setup

We'll carry out a free, comprehensive review of your existing IT — including your computers, servers, software, Microsoft 365, security, internet connectivity, phone systems, licensing and support arrangements.

We'll identify any risks, unnecessary costs or areas for improvement and explain our findings clearly and in plain English.

03

We take care of everything

Once you're ready to go ahead, we'll handle the onboarding — setting up monitoring, securing your devices, applying policies and making sure everything is running as it should.

We'll also work with your existing IT provider, internet supplier and other third parties to make the transition as straightforward as possible.

From day one, you'll have an IT support partner that understands your business and your setup.

Need Something Extra?

All of our packages can be extended with additional services tailored to your business. From ad-hoc on-site support to fully managed phone systems, we have you covered. Explore more below.

On-site support

We understand that sometimes things need an in-person touch. That's why we offer ad-hoc on-site IT support for when you need it most.

Our engineers are based around Surrey and London and can be at your place of business at a time that suits you.

Whether it's reconfiguring equipment after an office move or hands-on support that can't be done remotely, we've got you covered.

Network management

We monitor and manage your routers, switches, Wi-Fi access points and other infrastructure around the clock, keeping your connectivity stable, secure and performing at its best.

From flagging issues before they cause downtime to making sure your network is locked down and optimised.

We take the stress of networking completely off your plate.

Server management

Do you have servers on-premises or in the cloud? No problem — we'll take them off your hands and fully manage them for you.

We keep them secure, up to date and running smoothly because we know these servers often host critical software that keeps your business ticking.

We monitor them around the clock to make sure they're always in good health.

IT procurement

Do you require new laptops, desktops, licensed software or other business equipment?

We can help you procure exactly what you need, ensuring you're never left behind with old, outdated technology.

No more shopping from high street retailers or online marketplaces — we work with a wide range of distributors to bring you the right kit at a price that just makes sense.

VoIP & telephony

Gone are the days of analogue phones and clunky desktop handsets.

We set up and manage modern, flexible phone systems tailored to your business — and if you have existing numbers from legacy systems, we'll port them across for you free of charge.

From flexible softphones that let you take calls on the go to voicemail systems and auto-attendants customised to suit the brand and feel of your business, we've got your communications covered.

Bespoke needs

We cover a lot here at Staple, but we love a challenge.

If you have a bespoke system, product or service that needs looking after, we can still help.

Our engineers have worked with custom and niche solutions across a wide range of sectors, and even if something falls outside our remit, we'll work with you on a migration plan to a suitable alternative — taking the headache of IT away from you completely.
