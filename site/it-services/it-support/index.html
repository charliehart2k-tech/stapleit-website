Getting started is simple

From your first conversation to fully managed IT support, we make the whole process straightforward — no technical knowledge required.

01

Get in touch

Give us a call, send us an email or fill in our contact form and we'll get back to you.

We'll have a friendly, no-obligation conversation about your business, your current IT setup and what you actually need.

02

We audit your setup

We'll carry out a free, comprehensive review of your existing IT — including your computers, servers, software, Microsoft 365, security, internet connectivity, phone systems, licensing and support arrangements.

We'll identify any risks, unnecessary costs or areas for improvement and explain our findings clearly and in plain English.

03

We take care of everything

Once you're ready to go ahead, we'll handle the onboarding — setting up monitoring, securing your devices, applying policies and making sure everything is running as it should.

We'll also work with your existing IT provider, internet supplier and other third parties to make the transition as straightforward as possible.

From day one, you'll have an IT support partner that understands your business and your setup.
